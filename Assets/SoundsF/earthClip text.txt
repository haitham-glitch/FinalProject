Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. About 71% of its surface is covered by water, with the rest made up of continents and islands.

It has a protective atmosphere composed mainly of nitrogen and oxygen, which helps regulate temperature and shield life from harmful radiation.

Earth rotates on its axis once every 24 hours, causing day and night, and orbits the Sun once every 365 days, creating the seasons.
