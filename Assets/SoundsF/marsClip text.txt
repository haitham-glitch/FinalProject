Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the “Red Planet” because of its reddish appearance, caused by iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

It has a thin atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide and is much colder than Earth. Mars has the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, and deep valleys like Valles Marineris.

Scientists study Mars closely because it may have once had liquid water and could have supported microbial life in the past.
